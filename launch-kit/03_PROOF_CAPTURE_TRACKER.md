## 2. The metrics that matter (in priority order)

1. **3-second hold %** — target **70–80%**. This is the single most important
   number; it tells you if the grab works. Below 60% = fix the first frame.
2. **Median views** — judge the channel on the median, NOT the outliers.
   (Research: median AI TikTok = 418 views; the game is raising the floor.)
3. **Average watch time / completion** — target >40% for <15s, >30% for 15–30s.
4. **Saves + shares** — the strongest algorithm signals in 2026.
5. **Profile visits + follows** — the conversion path.

Ignore vanity spikes. One 400K post and nine 300-view posts is a median of 300.

---

## 3. Turning your own numbers into proof posts

When a post beats your median, it becomes a **Proof post**. Template:

> **[X] views. [Y] new follows. One post.**
> The hook: [the category/mechanism used].
> That's [category #] of 12. Free guide below.

**Screenshot capture:** native analytics screen, unedited, with the post visible
so viewers can verify it's real. Crop out anything private.

**Sequencing:** do NOT post a proof post about a video until it has at least 7 days
of data. Early numbers are noise.

---

## 4. Weekly review (every Sunday)

1. Fill in the tracker for the week.
2. Compute: median views, average 3s hold, best/worst category.
3. **Identify the best-performing category** → make 2 more posts in that category next week.
4. **Identify the worst** → retire that hook type or fix the first frame.
5. Pick next week's 5 posts accordingly.
6. If any post beat the median by 2x+, queue it as a proof post for the following week.
