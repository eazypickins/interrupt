# INTERRUPT — Creative Concept v2
## "WE INTERRUPT THIS FEED"

> This replaces the old creative direction. The practical setup (handles, sizes,
> bios) is retained at the end.

---

## 0. Why the old concept failed

The old assets (neon glitch flyer, chromatic-aberration logo, giant AI eye,
purple/orange gradients) are the **default AI-creator aesthetic of 2026**. In a
feed full of exactly that, they read as *expected* — so the brain files them as
scroll-past. That is the opposite of what INTERRUPT teaches.

The research backs this: consumer preference for AI-generated creator content
collapsed from **60% (2023) to 26% (2025/26)**, and the content that survives is
what *looks high-effort and different*. The old look was low-differentiation and
cold-neon — the visual definition of "AI slop."

**The fix: the brand must break the pattern of its own category.**

---

## 1. The concept

**The channel is a hijacked broadcast.**

INTERRUPT presents itself as a TV signal that keeps getting interrupted by
impossible footage. Every video opens like a broadcast being cut into. Every
surreal AI visual is framed as "footage" from a station that shouldn't exist.
The brand doesn't *describe* a pattern interrupt — it *performs* one, every time
you see it.

This is the meta-move no competitor can copy quickly: **your own brand identity is
a working demo of the product.**

---

## 2. Why it works (the strategy)

1. **It's the literal meaning of the name.** INTERRUPT = a signal interruption.
   The concept isn't decorative — it's the brand's dictionary definition.
2. **It's the aesthetic opposite of the feed.** The feed is cold neon, purple
   gradients, glossy 3D. This concept is **warm, analog, grainy, CRT, 1970s–80s
   public broadcast.** Different = interrupt.
3. **The intro IS a pattern interrupt.** A color-bar flash + static hit + "PLEASE
   STAND BY" is a legally-engineered attention grab. You demonstrate the product
   in the first 0.5 seconds of every post.
4. **It's infinitely expandable.** Test cards, color bars, tracking tears, REC
   overlays, lower-thirds, "dead air," sign-on/sign-off, "channel" numbers. A whole
   visual world, not one effect.
5. **It's legible at 40px.** Color bars and a bold condensed wordmark read at any size.
6. **It can't be mistaken for AI slop** because it's deliberately analog and warm.

## 3. The visual system

### 3.1 Palette — "Test Card"

| Role | Hex | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| CRT black | `#0B0B0C` | The default background. Off-black, never pure #000. |
| Bone / phosphor | `#EFE9DC` | Warm off-white. The "screen glow." Never pure white. |
| Signal | `#FF3B00` | The interrupt color. Red-orange, urgent, warm. |
| Test yellow | `#FFC400` | Bars + warnings. |
| Signal green | `#1FA97A` | Bars + "live" states. |
| Broadcast blue | `#2D6CDF` | Bars + cool contrast. |

**Rule:** the *core* look is **black + bone + signal**. The full 6-bar palette is
used **only** as the color-bar device (see 3.3). This keeps it disciplined, not
neon.

### 3.2 Type

| Use | Font (free) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Headlines / impact | **Archivo Black** or **Anton** | Heavy, condensed, utilitarian — broadcast signage |
| Labels / timecode / chyron | **IBM Plex Mono** or **Space Mono** | The "technical readout" voice |
| Body (rare) | **Archivo** | Clean grotesque |

**Drop Space Grotesk + the neon gradient entirely.** The new voice is a broadcast
control room, not a startup landing page.

### 3.3 The six signature devices (the brand's fingerprints)

1. **THE BARS** — a 6-color vertical bar strip (bone, yellow, signal, green, blue,
   black). The brand's "logo color." Appears on every asset: as an intro flash, a
   divider, a lower-third edge, a footer strip. Recognizable instantly.
2. **THE TEAR** — a horizontal wavy distortion band (a tracking error) that slices
   across images and the wordmark. This is the "interrupt" made physical.
3. **THE TEST CARD** — a circular broadcast test pattern (crosshair + bars + a
   countdown number). The intro frame and the avatar.
4. **REC ● + TIMECODE** — a persistent `● REC  00:00:00:00` overlay in IBM Plex Mono.
   Same corner, every post.
5. **STATIC / SNOW** — the transition. Any cut can be bridged by 4 frames of static.
6. **THE CHYRON** — a news-style lower-third bar for the category label:
   `CATEGORY 01 — GRAVITY & PHYSICS`.

## 4. The logo (new direction)

**Concept:** the wordmark **INTERRUPT** in heavy condensed grotesque (Archivo
Black), with a **horizontal TEAR slicing through the middle third** — the tear is
filled with static and a sliver of the color bars, slightly misaligned. The tear
*is* the interruption. It's meaningful, ownable, and reads at any size.

**Do NOT:** use chromatic aberration / RGB split (that's the old, generic look).

**Lockups:**
- Primary: `INTERRUPT` wordmark + tear, bone on black.
- Inverse: black on bone.
- Compact: `INT` + tear (for tiny spaces).

**How to build it (do NOT use AI image models for this):**
1. Canva or Figma.
2. Type `INTERRUPT` in Archivo Black, tight tracking, all caps.
3. Draw a horizontal band across the middle third (≈18% of the cap height).
4. Inside the band: a static/noise fill + a 4px sliver of the color bars.
5. Offset the band by ~2% so it reads as a misregistration, not a centered stripe.
6. Export SVG + transparent PNG (bone-on-black and black-on-bone).

**Tagline options (pick one):**
- `WE INTERRUPT THIS FEED` (the primary)
- `ENGINEER THE FIRST SECOND`
- `SIGNAL FOUND. PATTERN BROKEN.`

---

## 5. The avatar (the mark)

The wordmark is too wide for a square avatar. Use **the test card**.

**Spec:** black square → centered circular test pattern → crosshair + a ring of the
6 bars → a horizontal tear through the middle → a small countdown number or `● REC`
in the corner. Bone line-work, signal accent.

It reads as "broadcast" at 40px and is unmistakable next to every other avatar in
the feed.

---

## 6. The motion system (this is the scroll-stopper)

Every video follows the same broadcast grammar:

| Beat | Time | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| **Signal hit** | 0.0–0.5s | Black → bars flash → static burst + one percussive "interrupt" hit |
| **Test card** | 0.5–1.0s | Test card + `PLEASE STAND BY` in IBM Plex Mono |
| **The footage** | 1.0s+ | Snap to the impossible AI visual, framed with `● REC` + timecode |
| **Chyron** | 2–4s | Lower-third: `CATEGORY 0X — NAME` |
| **Teach** | 4–12s | Caption text (Archivo Black, bone, one signal-red keyword) |
| **Tear** | any cut | A tracking tear wipes between shots |
| **Sign-off** | last 1s | `WE INTERRUPT THIS FEED` + logo |

**The intro is not a logo animation — it is a live pattern interrupt.** That's the
entire strategic point. It works even on mute (the bars + test card are pure visual).

## 7. AI image prompts (for the creatives)

> **Text and exact graphics (bars, test card, logo) must be built in Canva/Figma —
> AI image models can't do them cleanly.** Use AI for the *photographic/textural*
> layer, then composite the graphics on top.

### 7.1 Broadcast texture background (for banners/covers)
```
A vintage CRT television test pattern background, warm analog broadcast aesthetic,
soft scanlines and film grain, muted bone-white and charcoal tones with a faint
orange phosphor glow, slightly dusty and imperfect, 1970s public television,
photographic, no text, [ASPECT].
```
**Negative:** `neon, purple, gradient, glossy 3D, modern UI, text, watermark, clean vector`

### 7.2 The impossible "broadcast footage" (for thumbnails + video visuals)
This is your product demo, now framed as hijacked footage:
```
[THE IMPOSSIBLE VISUAL — e.g. a red apple floating in mid-air] captured on vintage
broadcast video, warm CRT scanlines, film grain, slight tracking distortion,
phosphor glow, analog color bleed, shot on an old television camera, locked camera,
9:16 vertical, no text.
```
**Negative:** `neon, purple gradient, glossy 3D render, modern clean look, camera drift, text, watermark`

### 7.3 Static / snow texture (transition element)
```
Dense analog television static, black and white snow, heavy film grain, high
contrast, full frame, no objects, no text, [ASPECT].
```

### 7.4 Tracking tear element
```
A horizontal band of VHS tracking distortion and static, wavy analog signal tear,
bone-white and charcoal, isolated on a black background, [ASPECT], no text.
```

### 7.5 Banner compositions
Run 7.1 as the base, then in Canva add the wordmark + tear + tagline.
- **YouTube 2560×1440:** test-card pattern centered, wordmark left, bars along the bottom.
- **X / Bluesky 1500×500:** a horizontal strip of the bars, wordmark centered on black.
- **Facebook 820×312:** tear band across the middle, wordmark left, bars right.

### 7.6 Avatar
Do **not** generate the avatar with AI. Build the test card in Canva/Figma to spec
(§5). It must be pixel-precise and crisp.

## 8. How the concept applies to the 30 posts

Nothing in the post scripts changes — only the *skin*:
- Every post opens with the **signal hit → test card → footage**.
- The AI visual is presented as **broadcast footage** (REC + timecode + tear).
- The category label is a **chyron**, not a chip.
- Proof posts (analytics screenshots) are framed as **"EVIDENCE — FILE 0X"** with
  the REC overlay — real screenshots, broadcast framing.
- The CTA signs off with `WE INTERRUPT THIS FEED`.

**Net effect:** a feed full of neon AI channels, and one that looks like a rogue
1978 television station broadcasting impossible things. That's the interrupt.

## 9. Alternative directions (if broadcast doesn't fit)

If you want a different lane, two more concepts, same principle (be the opposite
of the feed):

- **"THE SPECIMEN"** — clinical/scientific. Stark white lab background, thin rules,
  specimen labels, crosshairs, `FIG 1: THE FLOAT`. Interrupts by being unnervingly
  *clean* among neon chaos. Type: a precise grotesque + mono.
- **"THE ERROR DIALOG"** — the brand as an operating-system error. `⚠ THE FEED HAS
  ENCOUNTERED A PATTERN IT CANNOT RESOLVE. [OK]`. Deadpan, dead-simple, funny.
  Type: system monospace.

All three share the winning mechanic: **look like nothing else in the feed.**
