# INTERRUPT — Channel Brand & Anchoring System

> **The core principle: one voice, one look, one signature, repeated forever.**
> A content farm varies everything. A brand repeats deliberately. Every element
> below is a *deliberate* repetition that turns a faceless AI account into a brand.

---

## 0. Positioning

**Channel promise:** "Engineer the first second."

**The proof loop (this is the whole strategy):**
This entire channel is generated with the system being sold. Every view count is
the proof. Each post = a demo (the grab) + a lesson (the value) + a receipt (the proof).
As the channel grows, its OWN analytics become the case study.

**Archetype:** Teacher / Proof Drop — the faceless-defensible lane
(research: only 1.1–1.3x penalty vs 2.5–4.5x for hot-take / talking-head formats).

---

## 1. The AI Voiceover (parasocial anchor)

**Rule: pick ONE voice and never change it.** This is the single most important
anchor. Viewers bond with a consistent voice faster than with a consistent face.

- **Tool:** ElevenLabs (recommended) or OpenAI TTS. If using Veo/Kling native
  audio, normalize the voice across posts (same model, same settings every time).
- **Settings to lock:** stability 0.55–0.65 · similarity 0.80 · style 0.30–0.40 ·
  speaker boost ON.
- **Voice character:** calm, deliberate, slightly low. *Engineered, not loud.*
  The brand promise is precision, not hype. No upspeak, no filler, no "hey guys."
- **Name it.** Refer to it internally as "the INTERRUPT voice." It is a brand asset.
- **Delivery rules:**
  - Short sentences. One idea per sentence.
  - A deliberate beat of silence right after the grab (0–2s) before the lesson.
  - End on a flat, confident down-note.
- **Captions carry the message for the ~85% watching on mute** — the voice is for
  the 15% who unmute and for the brand feel, not the primary information channel.

## 2. The Visual Signature

**Palette (already in your landing page — use it everywhere):**

| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Accent / CTA | `#FF6A00` (orange) |
| Brand | `#3B1F8F` (violet) |
| Background (dark) | `#1C1C1E` (charcoal) |
| Background (light) | `#FBFAF8` (paper) |

**Type:**
- **Space Grotesk** — headings and on-screen text slams
- **Inter** — body / caption text
- **JetBrains Mono** — prompt reveals (this makes the "here's the actual prompt" posts feel technical and credible)

**Fixed elements on EVERY post:**
1. **Intro sting (0.5s):** charcoal frame → "INTERRUPT**.**" wordmark (orange dot) → one sonic whoosh.
2. **Category chip (top-left, 2–4s):** `CATEGORY 0X — NAME`, orange text on charcoal.
3. **Caption style:** bottom-third, animated word-by-word, white text, **one orange keyword highlighted per line**, Space Grotesk.
4. **Outro frame (identical every time):** logo + "Engineer the first second." + CTA line.
5. **Aspect:** 9:16 vertical, consistent safe margins (keep text clear of the platform UI zones).

**The recurring motif:** the **floating / gravity-broken object**. It is your
visual signature. It appears in the intro, the outro, and the thumbnails, so the
feed recognizes you before it reads the handle.

---

## 3. Sound Design Signature

- **Intro:** 0.4s whoosh / sub-drop.
- **Bed:** low ambient, −22 to −18 LUFS. **Avoid trending audio** — it makes the
  channel timeless instead of trend-dependent, and it lets the visual carry the hook.
- **"Interrupt" cue:** one reversed/glitch hit *at the exact moment the pattern breaks*.
  Same cue, every post. This becomes your audio logo.
- **Outro:** same 1s tonal signature.

---

## 4. The Human Anchor (without showing a face)

1. **A named persona.** Even faceless, the account has a human voice in captions,
   comments, and DMs. Write in first person. Sign every caption the same way,
   e.g. `— G, INTERRUPT`.
2. **A recurring AI host character (optional, powerful).** One AI-generated
   narrator/character that appears across posts creates continuity and a
   "cast member" the audience recognizes. Use Runway Gen-4 for consistency.
3. **Reply to every comment for the first 90 days — as a human, first person.**
   This is the strongest faceless trust signal. No canned replies.
4. **Process posts.** Screen recordings of the actual workflow: prompt → generation
   → failed take → final. This is the "illusion of effort" made literal.

---

## 5. The "Illusion of Effort" (why it protects trust)

**Research:** when people perceive high effort (craft, iteration, collaboration),
they rate the content **+55% more trustworthy, +53% more innovative, +40% more
creative** — even when they know AI was involved.

**Show:** the prompt, the negative prompt, the failed take, the iteration, the choice.
**Never:** mass-templated output, identical B-roll with swapped text, zero craft.
**Rule:** every post must contain at least one visible proof of human judgment.

This is the antidote to "AI slop." The backlash targets *low effort*, not AI itself.

---

## 6. Consistency Rules (the checklist)

- [ ] Same voice, same settings
- [ ] Same palette, same fonts
- [ ] Same intro sting + outro frame
- [ ] Same caption style + category chip
- [ ] Same format structure (grab → teach → proof → CTA)
- [ ] Same posting rhythm
- [ ] Same sign-off
- [ ] At least one visible proof of human judgment per post

**If you change any of these, change them deliberately across the whole channel —
never on one post.**
