# Deep-Dive 05 — Material & Texture Swaps
## The Complete Sub-Technique Library (20 Prompt Variations)

> Part of the Scroll Interrupter Master System. Material swaps transform a
> subject into an impossible substance — sand, water, smoke, paper, glass —
> creating a strong, memorable reality violation.

---

## Why This Category Wins

The brain instantly identifies what something is *made of*. When a person is
made of flowing sand or a coffee mug is translucent glass, the material-
recognition system fails and the viewer stops to reconcile it. Material swaps
are also the most *aesthetic* interrupt — they produce shareable, "beautiful"
frames that people save and repost.

**Use this category when you need:** beauty, artfulness, premium feel, or a
visual metaphor (dissolving stress, melting anxiety, "made of" identity themes).

## Sub-Technique Library

### 5.1 — SAND / WATER / SMOKE PERSON (Material Morph)

**Prompt 1 — Sand Person:**
```
A [person] is made of flowing sand — their form constantly shifting, grains
pouring and cascading downward, the human silhouette recognizable but eroding
and re-forming. The sand behaves realistically. Dramatic lighting, locked
camera, 9:16 vertical.
```
**Negative:** solid flesh, rigid material, no particle motion, static texture.

**Prompt 2 — Water Person:**
```
A [person] is made of clear water — their form transparent and rippling,
droplets occasionally breaking off and rejoining, the silhouette fluid and
alive. The water refracts light realistically. Studio lighting, locked camera,
9:16 vertical.
```
**Negative:** solid body, opaque, no ripple, no refraction, static water.

**Prompt 3 — Smoke Person:**
```
A [person] is made of drifting smoke — their form soft and ethereal, wisps
curling and dissipating at the edges, the silhouette recognizable but
ever-shifting. Dark background, dramatic rim light, locked camera, 9:16.
```
**Negative:** solid form, no smoke, no drift, rigid, harsh edges, no ethereal.

**Prompt 4 — Liquid Metal Person:**
```
A [person] is made of liquid metal — chrome and reflective, their form
rippling and flowing, mirror-like surface catching light, the silhouette
smooth and morphing. Futuristic, cinematic lighting, locked camera, 9:16.
```
**Negative:** solid metal, no reflection, rigid, no flow, dull surface.

### 5.2 — PAPER COLLAGE / MIXED-MEDIA CUTOUT

**Prompt 5 — Paper Collage Falling (The Classic):**
```
Mixed-media paper collage: a [subject] is a recognizable full-color cutout with
a thin, uneven white paper border, moving with subtle natural motion over a
static [collage background of torn paper, photocopy textures, clouds, grain].
Analog zine imperfections, soft shadows between layers, locked camera, 9:16.
```
**Negative:** CGI, glossy 3D, clean vector, realistic person, no paper texture, text, watermark.

**Prompt 6 — Paper Collage Walking:**
```
Mixed-media paper collage: a [subject] walks through a [torn-paper city /
landscape], their cutout body moving with slight stop-motion, paper fibers and
imperfect edges visible, soft shadows under each layer. Warm analog aesthetic,
locked camera, 9:16 vertical.
```
**Negative:** smooth CGI, realistic, no paper texture, no shadows, clean vector.

**Prompt 7 — Scrapbook Scene:**
```
Scrapbook collage: [a scene] assembled from layered [photographs, magazine
clippings, handwritten notes, washi tape, pressed flowers], the subject a paper
cutout, moving subtly. Handmade, tactile, warm, locked camera, 9:16 vertical.
```
**Negative:** flat, no layers, no texture, glossy, digital, no handmade feel.


## Teaching Tip (For Your Product)

Material swaps teach one core lesson: **"what is it made of" is the brain's
fastest ID check.** Change the material, and you break the check. The skill is
choosing a material that *means something* — sand for eroding, water for
fluidity, smoke for fading, glass for fragility. Teach students: the material
is the metaphor.
