# Deep-Dive 06 — Mirror & Symmetry
## The Complete Sub-Technique Library (16 Prompt Variations)

> Part of the Scroll Interrupter Master System. Mirror interrupts exploit the
> uncanny — a reflection that misbehaves, an infinite regression, or a
> kaleidoscope that disorients. Symmetry and its violation are both hooks.

---

## Why This Category Wins

Mirrors are psychologically loaded: they're how we check our own identity. When
a reflection *doesn't match*, the brain registers a deep, primal "something is
wrong." Infinite symmetry, by contrast, is hypnotic — the eye gets caught in
the pattern. Both directions stop the scroll.

**Use this category when you need:** unease, mystery, identity themes, or
hypnotic, mesmerizing content.

## Sub-Technique Library

### 6.1 — INFINITE MIRROR (Recursion)

**Prompt 1 — Classic Mirror Recursion:**
```
Infinite mirror recursion: [a subject stands between two facing mirrors / holds
a mirror facing itself], creating an infinitely repeating reflection that
recedes into the distance. Each reflection is a slightly smaller copy.
Cinematic lighting, locked camera, 9:16 vertical.
```
**Negative:** single reflection, no recursion, flat mirror, no depth.

**Prompt 2 — Infinite Doorway:**
```
Infinite recursion: [a doorway] opens onto [a smaller identical doorway], which
opens onto another, repeating infinitely into the distance, each layer a
slightly smaller copy. Surreal, clean lighting, locked camera, 9:16 vertical.
```
**Negative:** single door, no recursion, flat, no depth, mismatched frames.

**Prompt 3 — Screen Within Screen:**
```
Infinite recursion: [a person holds a screen] showing [themselves holding the
same screen], repeating infinitely, each layer smaller, like a video within a
video. Clean, modern, locked camera, 9:16 vertical.
```
**Negative:** single screen, no recursion, flat, no depth, mismatched.

### 6.2 — REFLECTION MISMATCH (The Reflection Speaks Back)

**Prompt 4 — The Reflection Blinks:**
```
A [subject] looks into a mirror, and their reflection [blinks slowly when the
subject does not], a subtle uncanny mismatch. The subject is still while the
reflection acts. Realistic lighting, locked camera, 9:16 vertical.
```
**Negative:** reflection matching, no mismatch, cartoonish, exaggerated.

**Prompt 5 — The Reflection Smiles Wider:**
```
A [subject] looks into a mirror with a neutral expression, and their reflection
[smiles slowly and unnervingly wider], independent of the subject. Subtle,
creepy, realistic, locked camera, 9:16 vertical.
```
**Negative:** matching reflection, no smile, cartoonish, no uncanny feel.

**Prompt 6 — The Reflection Looks Away:**
```
A [subject] stares into a mirror, but their reflection [looks away / turns its
head / walks off] while the subject stays still, a deeply uncanny mismatch.
Realistic, muted lighting, locked camera, 9:16 vertical.
```
**Negative:** matching reflection, no independence, exaggerated, no uncanny.

**Prompt 7 — Delayed Reflection:**
```
A [subject] moves, and their reflection follows a beat too late, lagging
behind like a broken mirror. The subject is real-time while the reflection
trails. Uncanny, precise, locked camera, 9:16 vertical.
```
**Negative:** synchronous reflection, no lag, matching, no delay.

### 6.3 — KALEIDOSCOPE SYMMETRY

**Prompt 8 — Mandala Kaleidoscope:**
```
Kaleidoscope effect: [a subject/pattern] multiplied into radial mirrored
symmetry, rotating slowly, creating a hypnotic repeating mandala. Vibrant
colors, clean geometric precision, locked camera, 9:16 vertical.
```
**Negative:** asymmetric, single image, no mirroring, flat, static.

**Prompt 9 — Face Kaleidoscope:**
```
Kaleidoscope effect: [a face] mirrored radially into a trippy, symmetric
pattern, the features repeating and interlocking, slowly shifting. Surreal,
psychedelic, locked camera, 9:16 vertical.
```
**Negative:** single face, asymmetric, no mirroring, flat, no shift.

**Prompt 10 — Nature Kaleidoscope:**
```
Kaleidoscope effect: [flowers / leaves / natural patterns] mirrored into
radial symmetry, rotating slowly, creating a hypnotic organic mandala. Rich
color, crisp detail, locked camera, 9:16 vertical.
```
**Negative:** asymmetric, single image, no mirroring, dull, static.

### 6.4 — SPLIT-SCREEN IMPOSSIBLE

**Prompt 11 — Top/Bottom Mismatch:**
```
Split-screen impossible: the top half of the frame shows [one scene/subject]
and the bottom half shows [a completely different scene/subject], seamlessly
joined at a clean horizontal seam so they appear as one continuous but
impossible image. Locked camera, 9:16 vertical.
```
**Negative:** mismatched seam, broken join, two separate videos, no continuity.

**Prompt 12 — Two People, One Body:**
```
Split-screen impossible: [two different people] share one body at a vertical
center seam — one on the left, one on the right — moving and reacting in
unison as a single impossible person. Seamless join, locked camera, 9:16.
```
**Negative:** broken seam, mismatched, two separate people, no join, no unison.

**Prompt 13 — Half-Day / Half-Night:**
```
Split-screen impossible: one side of the frame is [bright day] and the other is
[deep night], joined at a clean seam, a single scene split between two times.
Surreal, seamless, locked camera, 9:16 vertical.
```
**Negative:** broken seam, both same time, no contrast, no continuity.

---

### 6.5 — SYMMETRY BREAK (One Detail Off)

**Prompt 14 — Perfectly Symmetric, One Thing Wrong:**
```
A [perfectly symmetrical scene / face] where a single small detail is
[asymmetrical / misplaced] — one eye slightly different, one object out of
place — creating a subtle, nagging wrongness the viewer must find. Clean,
locked camera, 9:16 vertical.
```
**Negative:** everything symmetric, no flaw, obvious flaw, no subtlety.

**Prompt 15 — The Copy Is Wrong:**
```
A [subject] stands next to [a duplicate of themselves], perfectly mirrored
except the duplicate [has one subtle difference — a different smile, a missing
detail, a wrong color]. Uncanny, precise, locked camera, 9:16 vertical.
```
**Negative:** identical copies, obvious difference, no uncanny, mismatched.

**Prompt 16 — Twin Symmetry Dance:**
```
Two [identical subjects] move in perfect mirrored symmetry, dancing or
performing a synchronized motion, one on each side of the frame, creating a
hypnotic visual. Precise, clean, locked camera, 9:16 vertical.
```
**Negative:** asymmetric, out of sync, mismatched, no mirror, broken timing.


## Teaching Tip (For Your Product)

Mirror interrupts teach one core lesson: **the reflection is the self, and
self-violation is the deepest uncanny.** The skill is choosing *how subtle* to
make the mismatch — a slow blink is creepier than a full turn. Teach students:
understated is scarier (and more scroll-stopping) than loud.
