# Deep-Dive 02 — Scale & Proportion Distortion
## The Complete Sub-Technique Library (18 Prompt Variations)

> Part of the Scroll Interrupter Master System. Scale distortion forces the brain
> to reconcile two incompatible size references — an instant "that's wrong" error.

---

## Why This Category Wins

The brain sizes up every object in a frame against its neighbors automatically.
When a strawberry is the size of a house, or a person shrinks while the world
stays fixed, the size-referencing system fails and the viewer stops to resolve
the contradiction. Scale interrupts are also highly *curious* — they make people
want to explore the impossible scene, which extends watch time past the hook.

**Use this category when you need:** curiosity, wonder, "cute" content, or a
product shown at impossible scale to emphasize its importance.

## Sub-Technique Library

### 2.1 — GIANT OBJECT / TINY SUBJECT

**Prompt 1 — Giant Fruit:**
```
A tiny [person] stands next to a [gigantic strawberry the size of a house],
emphasizing extreme scale contrast. Photorealistic, soft lighting, shallow
depth of field, dewy texture on the fruit. Locked camera, 9:16 vertical.
```
**Negative:** normal scale, proportional objects, flat, miniature without context.

**Prompt 2 — Giant Person / Tiny World:**
```
A [person] kneels over a [tiny city / tiny room / tiny landscape], their hand
larger than buildings, interacting gently with the miniature world below.
Photoreal, dramatic soft light, locked camera, 9:16 vertical.
```
**Negative:** normal scale, flat miniature, no depth, mismatched lighting.

**Prompt 3 — Oversized Product (Brand Focus):**
```
A [product, e.g., a lipstick / a sneaker] towers over a [person] like a
monument, the person looking up in awe, emphasizing the product's scale and
importance. Clean, cinematic, product-hero lighting, locked camera, 9:16.
```
**Negative:** product normal size, no scale contrast, dull, no hero lighting.

**Prompt 4 — Giant Everyday Object in a Room:**
```
A [normal object, e.g., a coffee cup] fills an entire [room], its base pressing
against walls, a [person] dwarfed beside it. Photoreal, consistent indoor
lighting, locked camera, 9:16 vertical.
```
**Negative:** object small, room not filled, no contrast, flat lighting.

### 2.2 — SHRINK / GROW (Continuous Scale Change)

**Prompt 5 — Person Shrinks:**
```
A [person] continuously shrinks in real time while the background stays
constant, going from normal size to [a few inches tall] within the same shot.
The world around them grows relatively larger. Smooth, gradual, physically lit,
locked camera, 9:16 vertical.
```
**Negative:** static size, sudden jumps, camera zoom, scale change on camera.

**Prompt 6 — Object Grows:**
```
A [small object, e.g., a seed] grows continuously in the palm of a hand, from
tiny to [filling the entire frame], while the hand stays the same size. Smooth
growth, natural motion, consistent lighting, locked camera, 9:16 vertical.
```
**Negative:** static size, jump cuts, hand shrinking, camera movement.

**Prompt 7 — Shrink-and-Restore Loop:**
```
A [person] shrinks to [tiny size] and then grows back to normal in a smooth,
continuous cycle, looping seamlessly, the world constant around them. Playful,
clean lighting, locked camera, 9:16 vertical.
```
**Negative:** hard cuts, broken loop, no size change, camera zoom.

---

### 2.3 — EXTREME ZOOM (Macro to Cosmic)

**Prompt 8 — Seed to Planet:**
```
An impossible continuous zoom: start at [a tiny seed in a hand] and pull back
continuously through [increasingly large scales — the hand, the person, the
field, the country, the planet] until reaching [the Earth seen from space].
Seamless, no cuts, smooth motion, 9:16 vertical.
```
**Negative:** jump cuts, jerky zoom, static framing, breaks in continuity.

**Prompt 9 — Face to Universe:**
```
An impossible continuous zoom: start at [a person's eye] and zoom out
continuously through [the face, the room, the building, the city, the planet,
the solar system] into [the galaxy]. Seamless, cosmic, smooth, 9:16 vertical.
```
**Negative:** jump cuts, breaks, static, no scale progression, blurry transitions.

**Prompt 10 — Macro into the Micro:**
```
An impossible continuous zoom INTO [an object], going from the visible surface
deeper and deeper into [fibers, cells, molecules], as if magnified beyond what
is possible. Seamless, scientific-marvel aesthetic, smooth, 9:16 vertical.
```
**Negative:** jump cuts, surface only, no inward zoom, blurry, broken continuity.

### 2.4 — MINIATURE WORLD (Tilt-Shift / Toy Effect)

**Prompt 11 — Tilt-Shift Farm:**
```
Tilt-shift miniature effect: [a farm with fields, a tractor, small figures]
rendered as a tiny, hyper-detailed toy model. Shallow depth of field,
exaggerated saturation, everything looks like a miniature diorama. Locked
camera, 9:16 vertical.
```
**Negative:** normal depth of field, realistic scale, full-size realism.

**Prompt 12 — Tilt-Shift City:**
```
Tilt-shift miniature effect: [a busy city street with cars and people] rendered
as a tiny toy model, with exaggerated saturation and shallow focus, moving like
a stop-motion diorama. Locked camera, 9:16 vertical.
```
**Negative:** normal scale, realistic depth, no toy feel, flat color.

**Prompt 13 — Toy Kitchen / Food:**
```
Tilt-shift miniature: [a kitchen scene with cooking] rendered as a tiny, cute
toy diorama, hyper-saturated, shallow depth of field, everything miniature and
charming. Warm light, locked camera, 9:16 vertical.
```
**Negative:** full-size realism, normal depth, no miniature feel, dull colors.

### 2.5 — IMPOSSIBLE CONTAINMENT

**Prompt 14 — Person in a Jar:**
```
A [person] sits curled inside [a large glass jar], looking out, the jar on a
table, the person impossibly small inside. Photoreal, warm light refracting
through glass, locked camera, 9:16 vertical.
```
**Negative:** person outside jar, wrong scale, broken glass, no refraction.

**Prompt 15 — Ocean in a Cup:**
```
A [cup / mug] contains an entire [ocean with tiny waves and a tiny boat],
impossibly vast inside the small vessel. Surreal, detailed, consistent lighting,
locked camera, 9:16 vertical.
```
**Negative:** flat water, no detail, wrong scale, empty cup, no vessel.

**Prompt 16 — Person Holding Their Own World:**
```
A [person] holds [a miniature version of their own world — their house, their
city, their life] in the palm of their hand, looking at it. Photoreal, emotional,
soft light, locked camera, 9:16 vertical.
```
**Negative:** flat, no detail, mismatched scale, no emotion, cluttered.

---

### 2.6 — STACKED SCALE (Recursive Self-Reference)

**Prompt 17 — Person Holding a Smaller Self:**
```
A [person] holds a [tiny version of themselves] in their palm, who in turn
holds an even tinier version, creating an impossible recursive scale stack.
Photoreal, gentle, locked camera, 9:16 vertical.
```
**Negative:** flat, no recursion, mismatched figures, no depth, creepy instead of charming.

**Prompt 18 — The Room That Contains Itself:**
```
A [room] contains [a miniature of itself on a table], which contains an even
smaller version, recursing infinitely — an impossible self-containing scene.
Surreal, clean lighting, locked camera, 9:16 vertical.
```
**Negative:** no recursion, flat, mismatched, single room, no depth.

## Negative-Prompt Cheat Sheet (Scale Category)

```
normal scale, proportional objects, flat, no depth, jump cuts, camera zoom
(instead of subject scaling), mismatched lighting, wrong proportions, hard cuts,
text, watermark, logo, play button
```

## Teaching Tip (For Your Product)

Scale interrupts teach one core lesson: **scale is relative, and the brain
checks it automatically.** The skill is choosing the *reference object* that
makes the mismatch obvious. A strawberry is only "giant" next to a person. Teach
students to always ask: "what is my size reference?" — then exaggerate it.
