# Deep-Dive 08 — Body & Anatomy Distortion
## The Complete Sub-Technique Library (15 Prompt Variations)

> Part of the Scroll Interrupter Master System. Body interrupts distort the
> human form — the thing the brain is most sensitive to — creating an instant,
> slightly uncanny reality violation.

---

## Why This Category Wins

The human body is the single most attention-privileged stimulus in the brain's
visual system. When anatomy breaks — extra hands, a swapped head, an elastic
limb — the brain *cannot* look away. Body distortion is also extremely
versatile: it can be comedic, uncanny, or wondrous depending on execution.

**Use this category when you need:** comedy, uncanny creepiness, or "impossible
ability" content (doing everything at once, multi-tasking, transformation).

---

## Sub-Technique Library

### 8.1 — DUPLICATED LIMBS / EXTRA HANDS

**Prompt 1 — Multi-Tasking Extra Hands:**
```
A [person] has [extra hands / extra arms] that move independently and
naturally, all performing [a task] simultaneously — cooking, typing, holding
items — while the main body stays calm. Realistic anatomy and lighting, locked
camera, 9:16 vertical.
```
**Negative:** missing limbs, extra limbs floating, distorted anatomy, wrong fingers.

**Prompt 2 — Four Arms Working:**
```
A [person] has four arms that each work on a different task at once, the extra
arms emerging smoothly from the shoulders, moving with natural coordination.
Surreal but anatomically lit, locked camera, 9:16 vertical.
```
**Negative:** arms floating, disconnected, distorted, wrong number of fingers, stiff.

**Prompt 3 — Extra Hands Holding Things:**
```
A [person] has several extra hands that each hold [a different object], the
hands emerging naturally and holding things steady while the person looks at
camera. Clean, precise, locked camera, 9:16 vertical.
```
**Negative:** floating hands, no connection, wrong anatomy, no grip, messy.

### 8.2 — HEAD / BODY SWAP

**Prompt 4 — Face on Wrong Body:**
```
Body-swap effect: [a face] appears on [a completely different body — an animal,
a child, an inanimate object], moving and reacting naturally. Seamless,
photorealistic, consistent lighting, locked camera, 9:16 vertical.
```
**Negative:** mismatched seam, floating head, wrong proportions, visible compositing.

**Prompt 5 — Animal Face on Human Body:**
```
Body-swap: [an animal's face] appears on [a human body], the expression and
movements matching, creating a surreal hybrid. Seamless, photoreal, consistent
lighting, locked camera, 9:16 vertical.
```
**Negative:** mismatched seam, floating, wrong proportions, uncanny valley excess.

**Prompt 6 — Two Faces Swapped:**
```
Body-swap: [two people] have swapped faces, each wearing the other's expression
and features on their own body, reacting naturally. Seamless, photoreal,
locked camera, 9:16 vertical.
```
**Negative:** mismatched seam, wrong proportions, visible compositing, uncanny.

---

### 8.3 — ELASTIC / STRETCHY BODY

**Prompt 7 — Rubber-Limbed Person:**
```
Elastic-body effect: a [person] stretches, squashes, and deforms like rubber —
limbs elongating, body compressing — then snaps back to normal. Cartoon
physics, smooth motion, locked camera, 9:16 vertical.
```
**Negative:** rigid body, no deformation, realistic bones, no squash and stretch.

**Prompt 8 — Long-Reach Arm:**
```
A [person] reaches out and their arm stretches impossibly long across [the
room / the frame] to grab [an object], then snaps back to normal length.
Cartoon physics, smooth, comedic, locked camera, 9:16 vertical.
```
**Negative:** rigid arm, no stretch, realistic, broken motion, no snap.

**Prompt 9 — Squash-and-Stretch Jump:**
```
A [person] jumps and their body stretches upward, then squashes flat on
landing, cartoon physics exaggerated, the motion bouncy and smooth. Comedic,
clean lighting, locked camera, 9:16 vertical.
```
**Negative:** rigid, no stretch, realistic gravity, no squash, stiff landing.

---

### 8.4 — DETACHED BODY PARTS

**Prompt 10 — Floating Hand:**
```
A [hand] detaches from a [person] and floats independently through the air,
performing [a task], while the person remains otherwise normal. The detached
hand retains realistic anatomy and lighting. Locked camera, 9:16 vertical.
```
**Negative:** blood, gore, horror, attached hand, no independence, floating without purpose.

**Prompt 11 — Head Detaches:**
```
A [person]'s head detaches and floats upward, looking around independently
while the body stays seated, then returns. Surreal, not gory, clean lighting,
locked camera, 9:16 vertical.
```
**Negative:** blood, gore, horror, attached head, no independence, grotesque.

**Prompt 12 — Eye Opens Where It Shouldn't:**
```
A [single eye] opens on [a surface / an object / a place where eyes shouldn't
be], blinking and looking around, surreal and uncanny but not gory. Clean,
precise lighting, locked camera, 9:16 vertical.
```
**Negative:** gore, horror, multiple eyes, no blink, grotesque, cartoonish.

---

### 8.5 — IMPOSSIBLE ABILITY (Body As Superpower)

**Prompt 13 — Person Splits Into Two:**
```
A [person] splits into [two identical copies of themselves] that step apart and
act independently, then merge back into one. Seamless, clean, locked camera,
9:16 vertical.
```
**Negative:** mismatched copies, no split, hard cut, no merge, visible compositing.

**Prompt 14 — Walking Through Walls:**
```
A [person] walks directly through [a solid wall] as if it were liquid, the wall
rippling around them, then they emerge on the other side. Surreal, seamless,
clean lighting, locked camera, 9:16 vertical.
```
**Negative:** wall solid, no ripple, person stopping, hard cut, broken.

**Prompt 15 — Body Folds Like Paper:**
```
A [person]'s body folds and unfolds like [origami / paper], creasing along
sharp lines, collapsing and re-expanding into a different pose. Surreal,
precise, clean lighting, locked camera, 9:16 vertical.
```
**Negative:** rigid body, no folding, realistic bones, no crease, hard cut.

## Negative-Prompt Cheat Sheet (Body Category)

```
floating limbs, disconnected parts, distorted anatomy, wrong number of fingers,
mutated face, mismatched seam, visible compositing, gore, blood, horror,
rigid body, realistic bones (for elastic effects), hard cuts, text, watermark,
logo, play button
```

## Teaching Tip (For Your Product)

Body interrupts teach one core lesson: **the human form is the strongest hook
in existence** — so violating it is the strongest interrupt. The skill is
controlling the *tone*: same extra-hands effect reads as "superhero" with
bright light or "horror" with dark light. Teach students: lighting and motion
set the emotional register, not the anatomy itself.
